## Known constraints
- Scanning a large OneDrive via Graph is slow (tens of thousands of items, paged).
  The explorer must scan progressively and cache results with a "last scanned" time.

## Known constraints (dev tooling)
- `npm audit` reports 5 high-sev advisories in a dev-only chain
  (braces→micromatch→fast-glob→@next/eslint-plugin-next), a glob DoS that only affects
  linting. `audit fix --force` would downgrade eslint-config-next, so left as-is.
